The Story

Three decades of original songs. One rule: make someone smile.

Sean Creighton is an American singer-songwriter who has been writing music for over three decades. He started performing in homeless shelters in Florida with a single goal — to make someone smile — and never stopped.

He has played his original songs on the streets of China, France, Sweden, Singapore, Germany, Costa Rica, Japan, and across the USA, earning an international following through free live-stream concerts during the pandemic and a devoted audience on the streets of China, where fans know him as the American who pours his heart out through song. His music is registered with BMI and copyrighted in the US Library of Congress.

Read the full biography

Sean was born in Portland, Maine, and picked up the guitar at 19, immediately writing his own songs. His influences include Stevie Ray Vaughan, Elton John, The Grateful Dead, Led Zeppelin, Walter Trout, B.B. King, and Sammy Hagar.

A multi-instrumentalist, Sean sings while playing electric guitar, acoustic guitar, ukulele, and piano keyboard. His live streams have drawn viewers from the United States, Europe, Brazil, South Africa, Nigeria, Uganda, Kenya, Dubai, Iran, Israel, Kazakhstan, and beyond.

Beyond music, Sean is a retired federal nursing officer and national expert investigator who has traveled to 65 countries — and he always brings his instruments. Many of his most popular songs were written abroad: "Baby, Baby, Baby We Could Be Good" was written walking the streets of Denmark, "I Miss You Like Crazy" while camping in Colorado, and "I Love the Way that You Love" on a cross-country road trip.

In 2021 his YouTube channel was monetized; in 2022 he became a verified Google Knowledge Panel member and began distributing his music to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and more through his distributor.

When he's not traveling, Sean calls the east coast of Florida home, where he swims, hikes, snorkels, and — of course — writes songs.
